Help the dogs

Tofs, my dog, joining the family is one of the best things that ever happened to me. Thanks to him I move more, I have a steady routine, and I’ve learned care and responsibility. Most of all, a dog is an endless source of joy and happiness.

Tofs made me pay more attention to dogs who were far less lucky: some were thrown out on the street by their owners, some had a paw hurt by a car, some got into a fight with aggressive dogs and lost an ear or an eye. Even one dog treated and fed is an incredible win.

For several years I’ve been helping dogs from shelters in Uzbekistan and Russia, and I know their founders, staff and volunteers personally. If you want to say thanks, help them: I know the money will reach them and the dogs will get proper care.

Who needs help

  • Mehr, Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Sponsoring one dog (shelter, care, treatment, food) costs 350,000 UZS a month. Payment details are on the shelter’s Instagram; more about the shelter in my channel (in Russian).
  • Sobaken, Ryazan, Russia. Sponsoring one dog (shelter, care, food) costs 11,500 RUB a month. More about the shelter and how to help in my channel (in Russian).

How you can help

  • Send money
  • Volunteer: walks and hands-on help
  • Adopt one of the dogs
  • Tell people who can help about the shelters
