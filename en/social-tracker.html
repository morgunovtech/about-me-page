Telegram bot · Open source

Social Tracker

Followers and account security in one Telegram bot.

A personal tool, shared as is: free and open source (MIT). The bot runs on your side — on your own server or on Railway — and messages you in Telegram.

What it does

  • Once a week, one report across Telegram, YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn: how many followers you gained over the day, the week and the month, with a 30-day chart.
  • Signs of a hijack: someone else’s link in your channel description or a new admin — a message within half an hour, with sound, even at night.
  • “New sign-in” and “password changed” emails reach you in Telegram within five minutes, not whenever you next open your mail.
  • Comments that get whole groups blocked by Telegram (drugs, scams, porn) — a card with “delete” and “ban” buttons. The bot deletes nothing on its own. You need your own paid Claude key, about a cent per comment.
  • Quiet while everything is fine: one message when something breaks, and one when it’s fixed.

Your data

The database stays on your server. Once a week the bot sends you a copy of it in the chat, without the keys. No analytics. The only things that leave are requests to the networks themselves and, if you turn on the comment check, the comment texts, to Claude.

What it costs

  • The bot is free.
  • Railway: a trial month, then $5 a month; you need a card that works for payments abroad.
  • On your own server: free.
  • The comment check: billed to your Anthropic account.

How to install

It takes 10–⁠15 minutes.

Step 1

Create a bot with @BotFather and copy its token.

Step 2

Press the Railway button — the form has one field, the token.

The link carries my referral code: your price is the same. Without the code: railway.com/deploy/social-tracker-bot.

Step 3

Press /start — the bot tells you where to begin. A Telegram channel connects with one button, the other networks with step-by-step pages.

You can also do it together with Claude: a skill that walks you through the setup right in the browser.

What this is, honestly

  • It’s not a service with support: if something breaks, you sort it out yourself, with the instructions.
  • The longest connections are Instagram and the security emails.
  • Telegram doesn’t give bots views or forwards — they stay in Telegram’s own statistics.
