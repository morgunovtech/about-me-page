Ways to work together

  • Product testing & reviews. I can try a device or service in my own workflows and explore what works well and what could be improved. If the experience makes for a useful story, I may write about it on my channels.
  • Early access & beta testing. I’m interested in early-stage products and new features in existing services. I can share feedback as both a user and a product manager, focusing on workflows, onboarding, UX and product decisions. An in-depth audit or ongoing work is consulting.
  • Expert & long-term partnerships. I’m open to developer communities, ambassador and expert programs, long-term testing, and partner programs for products I genuinely use or would like to use. We can agree on the scope and terms separately.
  • Interviews, research & media. I can contribute expert commentary, write a guest article, join a podcast or research project, or talk with founders about how and why a product came to be. My professional topics include product management, leadership, e-commerce, FinTech and emerging markets.
  • Speaking & professional communities. I’m open to talks, panel discussions, mentoring and judging. I’ve spoken at INMerge Tashkent, Click Data MeetUp and Managers Conf, mentored at the President Tech Award, and judged Startup Weekend and TechGenius.
  • Behind the scenes. I’m interested in seeing how products are made: meeting the team, visiting a factory or R&D facility, and learning about engineering and product decisions. If there’s a story worth telling, it may become an article.
  • Open-source project support. I build free, open-source tools for privacy, security and monitoring. I’m open to technical collaboration, infrastructure support and sponsorship of these projects.

How I work with partners

I test products in real-world scenarios and write about my own experience. I disclose paid collaborations, products provided for testing, and affiliate links. Providing a product does not in itself guarantee coverage or a positive review. We agree on the scope and terms in advance.

My content reflects my personal views, not those of my employer. I consider potential conflicts of interest before accepting a proposal.

Have something in mind?

Tell me what you’re working on and what kind of collaboration you have in mind. A product link and a little context help me give you a more useful response:

  • Who you are and what you’re building (with a link).
  • Why you think it might be a good fit.
  • The proposed format, timing and terms.
