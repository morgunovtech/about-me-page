About

I launch digital products in emerging markets — places where product, operations, and unit economics have to be assembled in parallel, fitted to the local context.

I consult companies in Russia, Uzbekistan, and Kyrgyzstan. Clients include UMAI Group, Belissimo Pizza, Buildex, MediaPark, and others.

Since 2009 I've launched four businesses — one sold to employees, three closed. Currently building two tools on digital privacy and running a Telegram channel about what I learn at work. I live in Tashkent with an Irish terrier named Tofs.
